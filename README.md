# XAUUSD Research

Quantitative research pipeline for XAU/USD 5-minute historical data.

## Goals

- Collect and validate historical market data
- Build reproducible research datasets
- Explore market patterns
- Formulate quantitative hypotheses
- Backtest strategies
- Perform out-of-sample and walk-forward testing
- Record experiments and results

## Current Status

Pipeline development.

No trading strategy has been implemented yet.
